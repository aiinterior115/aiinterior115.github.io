About us

AI INTERIOR is a Bangladesh-based interior design and construction company. We pair considered design with careful site execution, so the space you approve on paper is the space you receive.

We work with homeowners, business owners and developers. Every project starts by listening: how you live, how your team works, what you want to feel when you walk in.

The company is led by KH Galib Faysal, Proprietor, and is closely connected with the AI International Ltd. real estate project, AI INVICTUS.

  • Clear design Simple, balanced layouts in quality materials, using white, deep maroon and restrained gold.
  • Honest planning Defined scope, schedule and budget before work begins.
  • Careful finishing Supervised execution and a clean, complete handover.

Questions we often hear

Do you handle both design and construction?

Yes. One team plans the design and carries out the work, which keeps decisions and quality consistent.

What kinds of spaces do you work on?

Apartments, duplexes and homes, as well as offices, showrooms, restaurants and retail spaces.

How do I start a project?

Send us a message through the contact section. We will arrange a consultation to understand your space and requirements.

Can I choose my own materials and furniture?

Of course. We guide the selection and can also make furniture to measure.
